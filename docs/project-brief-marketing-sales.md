# EsportM Project Brief for Marketing and Sales

## Summary

EsportM is a club-first esports management platform for organizing players, squads, staff, matches, training, medical records, operations, marketplace recruiting, social publishing, and performance insights from one role-based workspace.

The platform is built for esports clubs and academies that need to move beyond scattered spreadsheets, chat groups, and manual admin work. EsportM gives each role a focused dashboard, while club admins and platform operators retain control over membership, permissions, subscriptions, and feature access.

## Positioning

EsportM helps esports organizations run like professional sports clubs.

It combines team operations, player development, match tracking, communication, and monetizable premium services in one platform. The commercial model supports free onboarding with upgrade paths for advanced analytics, AI assistance, medical management, social publishing, and marketplace recruiting.

## Pricing and Upgrade Model

The product supports a free-account-first model:

- Users can register and join or create club workflows before payment.
- Free clubs receive limited core access.
- Paid services stay visible but locked to encourage upgrade.
- Club admins are the primary upgrade decision makers.

Current plan structure:

- Free: entry plan for lead generation and basic club use
- Starter: expanded core limits for smaller clubs
- Professional: unlocks AI, advanced analytics, medical management, social publishing, and marketplace recruiting
- Elite: higher limits for larger organizations
- Enterprise: custom plan path for high-scale or managed deployments

## Buyer Personas

### Club Owner or Founder

Needs visibility into club operations, members, performance, and monetization readiness. Cares about control, professionalism, growth, and reducing manual coordination.

Sales angle: "Run your esports club from one operating system instead of spreadsheets and chat groups."

### Club Admin

Manages onboarding, roles, access, subscriptions, and member organization. Cares about clear permissions, clean workflows, and reducing repetitive admin tasks.

Sales angle: "Onboard, assign, manage, and govern every club member from one dashboard."

### Team Manager

Coordinates squads, matches, tasks, communication, and day-to-day operations. Cares about scheduling, accountability, and team readiness.

Sales angle: "Keep squads, matches, tasks, and staff aligned without losing context."

### Coach

Needs access to player profiles, training context, match history, and performance summaries. Cares about better decisions and player development.

Sales angle: "Turn match and training data into structured development decisions."

### Player

Needs a simple view of profile, assignments, schedule, stats, wellness, and opportunities. Cares about progress, visibility, and career growth.

Sales angle: "Build your player profile, track progress, and stay connected to your club."

### Physio or Health Staff

Needs controlled access to injuries, readiness, wellness, and recovery records. Cares about privacy, accurate tracking, and timely intervention.

Sales angle: "Give support staff the health context they need without exposing unnecessary club data."

### Recruiter or Agent

Needs searchable player listings and structured offer workflows. Cares about discovery, credibility, and transaction clarity.

Sales angle: "Find esports talent and manage offers through a dedicated marketplace workflow."
